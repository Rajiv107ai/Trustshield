# TrustShield AI — Model Card

**Model Version:** `v1.0.0-phase1-core`  
**Document Revision:** 2026-10-07  
**Model Types:** Ensemble (Random Forest + XGBoost + Heterogeneous Graph Neural Network + Unified Trust Engine)  
**Primary Frameworks:** Scikit-Learn 1.6+, XGBoost 2.1+, PyTorch 2.6+, NetworkX 3.4+, FastAPI 0.115+  

---

## 1. Model Details

### Architecture Overview
TrustShield employs a multi-tiered defense-in-depth architecture:
- **Phase 1 Tabular Baseline:** Random Forest / XGBoost trained on behavioral buyer/seller aggregates and price anomaly ratios.
- **Phase 3 Graph Augmentation:** NetworkX-computed topological metrics (PageRank, shared device/address connected component sizes, degree centrality) fused with tabular features.
- **Phase 4 Multimodal Detector:** Cosine similarity between CLIP text/image embeddings combined with seller age and listing pricing deviation to flag fake/mismatched listings.
- **Phase 5 Hybrid Deep Architecture:** 32-dimensional GNN buyer and seller node embeddings concatenated with Phase 3 feature vectors and scored via gradient boosting.
- **Unified Trust Engine:** Information-theoretic aggregator combining detector outputs into calibrated fraud risk, binary entropy confidence, detector disagreement, reason codes, and operational decision tiers (`ALLOW`, `REVIEW`, `HOLD`, `BLOCK`).

## 2. Intended Use & Application Scope

### Intended Applications
- **Real-Time Transaction Scoring:** Evaluating inbound orders for rapid velocity, account takeover, or synthetic identities at checkout.
- **Fraud Ring Identification:** Identifying coordinated collusion clusters sharing hardware identifiers, IP subnets, or delivery destinations.
- **Listing Integrity Verification:** Detecting counterfeit, fraudulent, or stock-photo mismatched product listings prior to catalog indexing.

### Out-of-Scope & Prohibited Uses
- **Autonomous Credit Scoring:** Not calibrated or legally permissible for credit underwriting or consumer loan decisions.
- **Sole Arbiter for Account Bans:** Transactions categorized as `HOLD` or `REVIEW` must route to trained human fraud analysts. Fully autonomous rejection without human oversight is prohibited.
- **Cross-Domain Transfer:** Model weights and graph topological priors are domain-specific to e-commerce marketplace behaviors.

---

## 3. Training Data & Temporal Split Protocol

### Data Generation
The models are trained and validated on synthetic marketplace transactions generated by the TrustShield simulation engine:
- **Entities:** 1,000 sellers, 10,000 buyers, 5,000 product catalog entries, device logs, address sharing logs.
- **Injected Fraud Scenarios:** Rapid-fire buyer velocity, synthetic identity clustering, buyer-merchant collusion rings, return fraud.

### Chronological Cutoffs (Zero Data Snooping)
- **Training Set:** Orders with `order_date <= TRAIN_END` (Days 1–45).
- **Validation Set:** Orders with `TRAIN_END < order_date <= VAL_END` (Days 46–60). Used strictly for threshold tuning, early stopping, and probability calibration.
- **Test Set:** Orders with `order_date > VAL_END` (Days 61–75). Frozen holdout used solely for final unbiased evaluation reporting.

## 5. Operational Considerations & Guardrails

1. **Cold-Start Entities:** Entities with $< 3$ prior transactions are explicitly flagged (`cold_start = True`). In accordance with epistemic uncertainty principles, their confidence score is attenuated by $30\%$, and zero-vectors replace historical embeddings.
2. **Anti-Feedback Loop Guard:** Model predictions and risk scores are explicitly banned from feature inputs to prevent model echo chambers and distribution drift.
3. **Detector Disagreement:** When component detectors diverge by $> 0.40$ in risk estimation, the transaction is flagged with `HIGH_DETECTOR_DISAGREEMENT` and prioritized for manual review.

---

## 6. Known Limitations & Caveats

- **Synthetic Data Disclaimer:** The system has been validated on realistic synthetic data. While behavioral patterns mirror known e-commerce attack vectors, production deployment requires fine-tuning on live historical chargebacks.
- **Static Graph Latency:** NetworkX graph computations scale with node count. In Phase 2, production real-time graph traversal will require distributed graph stores (Neo4j / Memgraph).
